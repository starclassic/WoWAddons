SCSeal

The native AuraContainer setup and numeric application formatter approach were
adapted from Unsealed Wrath Tracker 4.1.7 by KING (MIT), specifically Native.lua.
The display was reduced to a three-stack next-seal text/icon prompt. No audio,
art files, minimap code, or options panel from that addon are distributed here.
Glow.tga is an original procedurally generated gold border and halo texture.
Source: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/unsealed-wrath-tracker

credited there to talentsforever.com, via Pally Seal Cooldowns by Colby.
Source: https://talentsforever.com/data.json
